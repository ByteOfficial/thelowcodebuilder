Welcome to The Low Code Builder ("**we**," "**us**," "**our**," or "**the Site**"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website. ## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Contact Forms**: When you submit contact forms, we collect your name, email address, and message content.
- **Email Subscriptions**: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.
- **Comments**: Any comments you leave on blog posts may include your name and email.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Analytics Data**: Via Google Analytics, we collect information about your browsing behavior, including:
  - Pages visited
  - Time spent on pages
  - Device type and operating system
  - Referral sources
  - IP address (anonymized)

- **Cookies & Similar Technologies**: We use cookies to improve your experience. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

- **Log Data**: Our web server automatically records:
  - Access times
  - Pages accessed
  - Referring website

## 6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and track your usage.

### Types of Cookies We Use

- **Essential Cookies** - Required for basic site functionality
- **Analytics Cookies** - Track how you use the site (Google Analytics)
- **Preference Cookies** - Remember your settings

### Managing Cookies

You can:
- Disable cookies in your browser settings
- Use incognito/private browsing mode
- Clear cookies manually

**Note:** Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

## 12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to:

- Know what personal information is collected
- Know whether it's sold or shared
- Delete personal information
- Opt-out of the "sale" of personal information

**Note:** We don't sell your data, but these rights still apply.

---

## 13. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you're in the EU, you have rights under GDPR, including:

- **Right of Access** - Get a copy of your data
- **Right to Rectification** - Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure** - Delete your data ("Right to be Forgotten")
- **Right to Restrict Processing** - Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Data Portability** - Receive data in portable format
- **Right to Object** - Opt-out of certain processing
