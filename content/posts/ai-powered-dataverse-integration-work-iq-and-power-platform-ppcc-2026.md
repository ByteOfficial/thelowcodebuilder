## The Solution: AI That Talks Your Data’s Language

### Real-Time Sync with the AI Business Connector API

At the heart of the update is the **AI Business Connector API**, which enables **bidirectional synchronization** between Work IQ’s AI insights and **Dataverse entities**. This means AI-generated forecasts, recommendations, and corrections can update your data models in real time — and vice versa. For example, if your AI model predicts a 20% drop in sales for a product category, the API automatically updates the relevant Dataverse records, triggering alerts in Power Apps or Power BI.

### Dynamic Schema Updates with Prompt Columns 2.0

Gone are the days of manually updating data models when your business evolves. **Prompt Columns 2.0** now support **dynamic schema updates**. Let’s say your sales team starts tracking a new metric like "Customer Lifetime Value." Instead of waiting for an IT admin to modify the data model, Prompt Columns 2.0 use AI to detect the new field, auto-generate the schema, and apply data validation rules. This cuts deployment time by **60-80%** for ISVs, as shown in pilot programs with Fortune 500 companies.

### Natural Language Querying with Dataverse Skills v3

What if your team could ask questions in plain English and get answers from your data? **Dataverse Skills v3** brings **natural language querying** to Power Platform. Sales reps can now type, "Show me this quarter’s pipeline by region," and the system returns a filtered view of the data. Under the hood, it uses **improved NLP with 92% entity recognition accuracy** and even supports **complex SQL-like queries** via the **Power Automate AI Translator**. This means no more fumbling with filters or writing SQL — just speak your mind.

## The AI Cache Layer: Speed Without Sacrificing Accuracy

One of the biggest wins for enterprises is the new **AI Cache layer** in Dataverse. When Work IQ processes large volumes of data — like generating forecasts for 10,000+ sales opportunities — the AI Cache acts as a **dedicated memory layer** to reduce latency. Think of it as a buffer that stores intermediate AI results, so your Power Automate flows and Power BI dashboards load instantly. This is a game-changer for makers who previously faced performance bottlenecks during high-volume AI operations.
